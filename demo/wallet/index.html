WebMCP capabilities exposed by this page

    Buying is two calls, never one: get_quote prices it and moves nothing, execute_quote spends. A quote expires, and an idempotencyKey makes a repeated call return the original trade instead of buying twice.

    Not exposed to agents: withdraw, add_withdrawal_address, create_api_key. Moving money off this wallet is something you do yourself.
